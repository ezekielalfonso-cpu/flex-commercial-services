Our Story

Live now, clean less.

David George and Riley Hinkel started Flex Services the way most good things start, with years of actual work. Not from a boardroom, but from restaurant kitchens and office hallways. They saw firsthand how a truly professional clean could transform a business environment, and they built Flex because they knew they could do it better.

Every job at Flex gets that same personal attention to detail that David and Riley brought from day one. We're not a franchise operation. We're a Northeast Ohio business that takes pride in the community we serve.

From medical sanitization and routine office maintenance to post-event cleanup, Flex Commercial Services delivers professional cleaning done right, every time.
